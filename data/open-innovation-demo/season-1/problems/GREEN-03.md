## Problem Statement

Campus cafeterias overproduce food because meal demand changes daily. Design a system that forecasts demand, flags likely surplus early, and routes usable excess inventory to partner kitchens before it is discarded.

## Expected Solution

Teams should propose forecasting inputs, operational decision rules, and a simple dispatch view for staff or volunteers.
