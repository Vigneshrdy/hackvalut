## Problem Statement

During emergencies, the city must publish updates quickly in several languages. Build a coordination layer that assigns translators, tracks approval status, and prevents outdated translations from being published after the source message changes.

## Expected Solution

The proposed system should show message lineage, assignment status, and publication readiness across all active languages.
