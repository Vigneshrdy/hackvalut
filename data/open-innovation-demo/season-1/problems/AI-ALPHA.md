## Problem Statement

Scholarship reviewers need help sorting a large pool of applications without turning the process into a black box. Build a tool that highlights comparable candidates, summarizes review notes, and surfaces conflicts or missing evidence for a final human decision.

## Expected Solution

The solution should include transparent scoring explanations, reviewer workflows, and clear audit trails for every recommendation.
